Ontological Agnosticism & Ultimate Speed

Much of modern physics is bogged down in unresolvable debates about the true nature of reality—whether the universe is a probability wave, a string, or a hologram. The Anastasov Field Medium Initiative bypasses this philosophical gridlock entirely through strict computational pragmatism.

Our guarantee is simple: Regardless of what the universe is, our continuous-variable kinematic engines calculate what it does faster than any other known framework. By replacing abstract multidimensional tensors and probabilistic wavefunctions with $O(N^2)$ deterministic hydrodynamics, we deliver the exact same empirical numbers at a fraction of the computational cost.

Operating at a time of unprecedented open-source scientific enthusiasm, this repository serves as a centralized archive for computational physics frameworks that push the boundaries of established science. Standing firmly on the shoulders of the Newtonian giants who laid our mathematical foundations, our mission is not to discard their legacy, but to boldly optimize it. By integrating the strict Quality Assurance (QA) standards, high-throughput testing, and data-driven methodologies of the modern IT revolution, we seek faster, more stable, and highly predictable mechanical solutions to the universe's most complex problems.

We do not guess whether Schrödinger's cat is dead or alive in a probabilistic superposition. Instead, we leverage the raw power of modern continuous-variable computing to look inside the box—to calculate exactly when it drank the milk, where it scratched the walls, and the precise trajectory it will take the moment the lid is opened. This is physics processed through the lens of extreme engineering.

Rather than challenging the empirical validity of standard models, this project advocates for epistemic pluralism—the active maintenance of multiple concurrent theoretical ontologies to process data. But philosophical pluralism is meaningless without raw empirical validation. By treating the laws of classical physics as a strict computational engine subject to modern QA standards, we bring these theories out of the abstract and into the code.

By offering these models as a rigorous intellectual stress-test, we demonstrate that astronomical phenomena, subatomic NIST benchmarks, and massive QM9 chemical databases can all be accurately reproduced through classical fluid kinematics (which essentially operates as an applied vector calculus framework), vector dynamics, and hydrodynamic drag. We treat the vacuum as a polarizable "Field Medium." Crucially, this is not a mystical 19th-century aether; it is simply the aggregate of all interacting fields—the underlying equations and cumulative effects that make up the "software" calculating the universe. By operating strictly within this flat Euclidean space, we push the boundaries of deterministic mechanics to their absolute computational limit.
