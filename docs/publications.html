Ontological Duality in Weak-Field Gravity: Euclidean Field Relativity

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20531614

This paper formalizes Euclidean Field Relativity (EFR), demonstrating a strict algebraic isomorphism with the post-Newtonian limit of General Relativity. By modeling gravitational potential as variable medium impedance, geometric predictions are recovered using flat-space vector kinematics. The framework includes a raw data audit of archival white dwarf spectra (VLT/UVES WD 0738-172), establishing macroscopic parity with GR (+34.430 km/s) and setting a rigorous observational upper bound of $|\Delta\alpha/\alpha| \le 4.1 \times 10^{-6}$ for testing element-specific quantum divergence.

Keywords: General Relativity, Astrophysics, VLT/UVES, Kinematics, Field Medium, Orbital Precession

The Rapid Alignment Kinematic Theory of Spin (RAKTS)

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20531698

The Rapid Alignment Kinematic Theory of Spin (RAKTS) replaces abstract probability wavefunctions with localized, continuous energy streams interacting within a resistive Field Medium. The model achieves mathematical parity with standard Quantum Mechanics outcomes through deterministic fluid-dynamic mechanics. Key benchmarks reproduced classically include the derivation of the 109.5° tetrahedral bond angle for Methane, matching NIST IR vibrational frequencies, and simulating the Stern-Gerlach split and Frisch-Segrè (1933) transitions without probabilistic superposition.

Continuous-Variable Kinematic Computing: A Hydrodynamic Architecture

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20821520

This paper proposes a complementary analog computing architecture inspired by the Rapid Alignment Kinematic Theory of Spin (RAKTS) framework. Why Students Deserve More Than One Theory of Gravity: Underdetermination and the Pedagogy of Modern Physics

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20789600

The underdetermination of theory by data is one of the most established principles in the philosophy of science. Yet modern physics education proceeds as though underdetermination does not exist. Graduate students learn General Relativity as the singular correct description of gravity, largely unaware that multiple alternative frameworks—built on entirely different ontological foundations—reproduce the same classical predictions to identical precision. This paper examines the pedagogical consequences of this omission and proposes the integration of computational model-comparison exercises (using frameworks like RAKTS) into graduate education as a practical remedy.

Keywords: Underdetermination, Duhem-Quine, General Relativity, Physics Education, Epistemology, RAKTS

The Relativity of Prime Numbers: Grid-Dependent Distributions and Cryptographic Applications

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20819893

This paper explores an alternative epistemological perspective on number theory: that the conventional notion of primality is relative to the choice of algebraic structure, rather than an intrinsic universal constant. Consistent with a continuous "Field Medium" ontology, we demonstrate via alternative multiplicative structures (such as Hilbert monoids and numerical semigroups) that both absolute values and relative index mappings of prime numbers are grid-dependent artifacts. Building on this foundation, the paper introduces Dynamic Monoid Chaining (DGC v2) as an open problem in cryptography, proposing a shifting cryptographic architecture designed to render parallel quantum factorization structurally ineffective.

Keywords: Number Theory, Post-Quantum Cryptography, Dynamic Monoid Chaining, Hilbert Monoids, Shor's Algorithm, Open Problem, Epistemic Pluralism, Numerical Semigroups
