Ontological Duality in Weak-Field Gravity: Euclidean Field Relativity

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20531614

This paper formalizes Euclidean Field Relativity (EFR), demonstrating a strict algebraic isomorphism with the post-Newtonian limit of General Relativity. By modeling gravitational potential as variable medium impedance, geometric predictions are recovered using flat-space vector kinematics. The framework includes a raw data audit of archival white dwarf spectra (VLT/UVES WD 0738-172), establishing macroscopic parity with GR (+34.430 km/s) and setting a rigorous observational upper bound of $|\Delta\alpha/\alpha| \le 4.1 \times 10^{-6}$ for testing element-specific quantum divergence.

Keywords: General Relativity, Astrophysics, VLT/UVES, Kinematics, Field Medium, Orbital Precession

The Rapid Alignment Kinematic Theory of Spin (RAKTS)

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20531698

The Rapid Alignment Kinematic Theory of Spin (RAKTS) replaces abstract probability wavefunctions with localized, continuous energy streams interacting within a resistive Field Medium. The model achieves mathematical parity with standard Quantum Mechanics outcomes through deterministic fluid-dynamic mechanics. Key benchmarks reproduced classically include the derivation of the 109.5° tetrahedral bond angle for Methane, matching NIST IR vibrational frequencies, and simulating the Stern-Gerlach split and Frisch-Segrè (1933) transitions without probabilistic superposition.

Keywords: Quantum Mechanics, Fluid Dynamics, Stern-Gerlach, Molecular Geometry, Kinematics, Determinism

Continuous-Variable Kinematic Computing: A Hydrodynamic Architecture

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20583015

This paper proposes a complementary analog computing architecture inspired by the Rapid Alignment Kinematic Theory of Spin (RAKTS). Rather than attempting to isolate discrete probability states from a noisy environment, this architecture embraces the environment as a continuous computational medium. By modeling information not as probabilistic amplitudes, but as continuous geometric "Vector Nodes" subject to physical hydrodynamic drag (Landau-Lifshitz damping), we outline the theoretical foundation for an Analog Topological Processor, establishing Turing completeness via a simulated universal Kinematic NAND Gate.

The Relativity of Prime Numbers: Grid-Dependent Distributions and Cryptographic Applications

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20531812

This paper challenges the foundational status of prime numbers in number theory, presenting them as coordinate-dependent artifacts of an arbitrary discrete measurement system rather than universal constants. Consistent with the continuous "Field Medium" paradigm, we demonstrate through non-unitary Hilbert monoids that absolute values and relative sequence indices shift completely based on the observer's step size. Introduces Dynamic Grid Cryptography (DGC) as a novel cryptographic framework that avoids standard integer-based quantum decryption algorithms (Shor's Algorithm).

Keywords: Number Theory, Prime Numbers, Cryptography, Shor's Algorithm, Quantum Decryption, Epistemic Pluralism, Hilbert Monoids
