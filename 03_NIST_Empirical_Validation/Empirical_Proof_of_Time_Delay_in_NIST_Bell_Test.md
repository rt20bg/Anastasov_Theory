They are the topological negative space. From this perspective, prime numbers are simply **artifacts of the cutting process**. The "grid" and the "cutter" dynamically create the primes. Without the act of sieving, the primes do not exist as isolated entities; they are defined entirely by the structural boundaries of the cuts.
