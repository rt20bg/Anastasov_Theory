# RAKTS: Proposed Falsification Experiments (Quantum Kinematics)

## 1. The High-Density Electron Beam Paradox
Modern Quantum Mechanics (QM) dictates that large-scale diffraction patterns are the result of individual wave-function probabilities.

### The Experiment:
Perform a standard electron diffraction test but increase the beam intensity to extreme levels. In this regime, the Coulomb repulsion (electrostatic "pushing") between the electrons should be massive.

### Predictions:
*   **Orthodox Physics (QM):** The intense mutual repulsion and entropy will lead to "Decoherence." The interference pattern will blur and eventually collapse into a chaotic noise.
*   **RAKTS Kinematics:** The pattern will remain **stable and clear**. 
    *   **Reasoning:** In RAKTS, the diffraction is not a property of a "probabilistic wave," but a mechanical result of the crystal lattice acting as a **physical sieve**. Like sand falling through a screen, the density of the sand does not change the pattern of the screen.

## 2. The "Electron Mutation" Catalog
Is an electron always the same object? Standard physics says yes (indistinguishable particles). RAKTS suggests the "electron" is a localized kinematic vortex whose mass and charge are determined by the local pressure of the Field Medium.

### The Experiment:

1.  Light atoms (e.g., Hydrogen) vs. Heavy atoms (e.g., Lead).

3.  High-energy plasma vs. low-temperature superconductors.

### Goal:
Create a "Genetic Catalog" of the electron. If we find even a 1 part per billion (ppb) variance in mass or charge based on the parent atom, the concept of "Fundamental Indivisible Particles" is falsified.
